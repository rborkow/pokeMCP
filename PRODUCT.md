# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

PokeMCP Prep is for improving Pokémon Champions ladder players who want to turn public
tournament information into a practical plan for their next set. They may not know an opponent's
exact spreads, but they can recognize teams, paste a team sheet, and practice specific openings.

## Product Purpose

The product makes tournament preparation concrete. It starts with sourced event results and team
sheets, then combines a player's team with an indexed, pasted, or manually entered opponent to
produce a saved battle card. Success is measured by weekly completed prep plans: both teams,
generated battle card, and a save or export.

## Positioning

The shortest path from the current Champions metagame to a matchup plan you can practice.

## Brand Personality

Calm, informed, and practical. The voice resembles a thoughtful tournament desk and a concise
coach: evidence first, candid about uncertainty, and focused on decisions the player can rehearse.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble an RPG quest log, neon esports HUD, generic AI terminal, or fantasy battle screen.
Avoid giant versus treatments, glowing containers, fake win probabilities, and unexplained model
confidence. The interface must not make sourced facts and AI suggestions look equivalent.

## Design Principles

- Start from evidence and show its date and source.
- Turn analysis into a reusable preparation artifact, not a transient conversation.
- Put the matchup decision first; keep general team generation secondary.
- Mark incomplete Champions mechanics at the exact recommendation they affect.
- Keep anonymous preparation complete and make accounts an optional continuity layer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, support keyboard-only use and visible focus, remain usable at 320px, avoid
color-only status communication, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
