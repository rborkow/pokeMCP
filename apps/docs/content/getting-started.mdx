# Getting started

## 1. Add your team

Open **[Build a team](https://www.pokemcp.com/build?mode=grid)** to import a Showdown-style paste or edit your current Champions team. Include moves, items, and abilities when you know them; the plan becomes more specific as the team sheet becomes more complete.

## 2. Choose an opponent

From the newsroom, open a completed event and select **Prepare against team**. You can also start from **[New prep](https://www.pokemcp.com/prep/new)** and paste a team sheet or enter Pokémon names manually.

## 3. Read the battle card

The Match Desk separates:

- The recommended Bring 4.
- A primary and alternative lead pair.
- Likely opposing leads to rehearse.
- Opening-line principles and danger points.
- A practice checklist.
- Evidence and mechanics limitations for each recommendation.

Likely leads are rehearsal targets, not win-rate predictions. VP-sensitive speed order and incomplete Champions move interactions are marked as beta where they matter.

## 4. Save or export

Anonymous plans stay in your browser. Copy the card as Markdown, print or save it as PDF, and download a JSON workspace backup from **Teams**. Optional Discord or Google sign-in merges the newest copy of each saved item into your account.
