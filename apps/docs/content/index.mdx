# PokeMCP Prep

PokeMCP Prep is a **Champions tournament-preparation workspace** for players who want a practical plan for the teams they are seeing on ladder and in events.

## What it does

- Publishes a sourced newsroom of completed public Champions events.
- Lets you choose an indexed team, paste a team sheet, or enter the known Pokémon manually.
- Produces a battle card with Bring 4, two lead options, likely opposing leads, opening lines, danger points, and practice work.
- Labels tournament records, calculations, coach suggestions, and incomplete Champions mechanics separately.
- Works fully without an account; Discord or Google sign-in is optional for sync.

## Start preparing

Visit **[www.pokemcp.com](https://www.pokemcp.com)** and choose a published team from the newsroom, or go directly to **[New prep](https://www.pokemcp.com/prep/new)**.

The former public MCP and REST tool surfaces have been retired. Their analysis capabilities now support the Prep product internally.
