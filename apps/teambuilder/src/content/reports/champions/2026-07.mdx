## Where the meta stands

The format has no runaway dominator, but a tight cluster of four Pokémon sit above the 28% line: **Sinistcha (33.00%)**, **Garchomp (32.30%)**, **Basculegion (30.34%)**, and **Incineroar (28.35%)**. Sinistcha and Garchomp anchor the top, with Basculegion's Choice Scarf offense close behind and Incineroar remaining the format's default pivot/intimidate body. **Whimsicott (26.67%)** rounds out the high-usage tier as the premier redirection/Tailwind speed-control option.

The mid-tier is dense and Mega-driven. **Kingambit (23.09%)** and **Farigiraf (21.61%)** are both strong Trick Room/support-adjacent picks, and from there usage compresses quickly — **Charizard-Mega-Y (15.32%)**, **Staraptor-Mega (15.14%)**, **Floette-Mega (14.90%)**, **Swampert-Mega (14.10%)**, **Metagross-Mega (12.96%)**, and **Mawile-Mega (10.32%)** all cluster within a few points, reflecting a format where Mega selection is a genuine team-identity choice rather than a settled question. Weather support (**Pelipper 17.99%**, **Torkoal 10.25%**) confirms rain and sun archetypes are both live.

Tournament data sharpens the ladder picture. Across three events, the winning cores lean on a repeated **Charizard-Y / Farigiraf / Garchomp / Sylveon** backbone (JoeUX9 and Louie John Valencia both ran four of those). Incineroar and Kingambit were the two most-brought Pokémon in the St. Jude top 16 (9/16 each), and Kingambit led the Khun top 8 at 6/8 — a stronger showing in cut than its raw 23.09% usage suggests.

## Where it's heading

Only one month is on record for Reg M-B (2026-06), so there is **no month-over-month trend to extrapolate** — every "series" here is a single data point. I will not fabricate directional momentum from one snapshot. What we *can* read is the gap between ladder usage and tournament performance:

- **Kingambit** and **Charizard-Y** are outperforming their ladder rank in cut (Kingambit 9/16 and 6/8; Charizard 7/16 and 5/8 despite only 15.32% ladder usage). If ladder follows results, expect both to climb next month.
- **Sylveon (12.82%)** and **Farigiraf (21.61%)** both appeared on multiple winning teams and should hold or rise as the "Fairy Feather + Trick Room support" package proves out.
- **Aerodactyl-Mega** does not appear in the top 20 at all yet was brought by both non-Charizard winners' partners — a candidate to enter the table if its tournament presence is real.

Treat all of the above as extrapolation from a thin one-month sample plus tournament signal, not as established trends.

## What to prepare for

- **Build to beat the Charizard-Y / Farigiraf / Garchomp / Sylveon core.** It won two of three listed events. You need answers to sun-boosted Charizard and Life Orb Garchomp simultaneously, plus a plan against Sylveon's Fairy pressure and Farigiraf's support tools.
- **Respect Kingambit as a cut-defining threat.** It's a top-two bring in both major events. Spread-move users and Fairy/Fighting coverage stay premium; don't let Sucker Punch and Kingambit's Supreme Overlord snowball go unpunished.
- **Have redundant speed control.** With Whimsicott (26.67%) on Tailwind, Farigiraf (21.61%) enabling Trick Room, and Basculegion (30.34%) on Choice Scarf, the format swings between speed modes. A team that only functions in one gear will get read.
- **Cover both weather archetypes.** Pelipper (17.99%) and Torkoal (10.25%) mean rain (Basculegion, Archaludon at 15.80%) and sun (Charizard-Y, Venusaur) are both live; a hard weather-dependent build is exposed to the mirror-matchup coin flip.

## Common questions

### Is Sinistcha good in Regulation M-B?

Yes — it's the single most-used Pokémon at **33.00%**, and it appeared in DonVGC's INGAGE-winning team on a Kasib Berry (3/8 top-cut bring rate there). Its ladder dominance combined with a tournament placement makes it a safe, well-established pick rather than a fad.

### What is the best Charizard-Y team in Champions?

The winning template pairs Charizard-Y with **Farigiraf, Garchomp, and Sylveon** — that four-Pokémon backbone won both the St. Jude and Khun events, differing mainly in the fourth/fifth slots (Venusaur + Incineroar vs. Aerodactyl-Mega + Kingambit). Charizard's ladder usage is only 15.32%, but its 7/16 and 5/8 top-cut bring rates show it punches well above that in high-level play.

### How strong is Kingambit right now?

Stronger in tournaments than on ladder. Its 23.09% usage ranks 6th, but it was tied for the most-brought Pokémon at St. Jude (9/16) and led the Khun top 8 (6/8), signaling it's a premium cut pick. Expect ladder usage to catch up if these results hold.
