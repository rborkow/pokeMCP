## Where the meta stands

The top of the ladder is still anchored by the familiar trio: **Sneasler at 35.63%** (-1.46), **Rillaboom at 35.52%**, and **Incineroar at 31.83%** (+1.16). But the headline is Rillaboom's collapse — down 10.44 points from a dominant 45.96% in September. It's still #2, yet it no longer sits in a tier of its own. **Kingambit (26.63%, +2.37)** rounds out the core support/attacker cluster.

The real story of October is the sun matchup surging into the picture. **Charizard-Mega-Y nearly doubled to 21.59% (+10.68)** and **Garchomp more than doubled to 19.54% (+11.04)**, the two biggest risers in the format. Meanwhile the previous premier Mega, **Salamence-Mega, cratered from 27.10% to 12.37% (-14.72)** — the single largest fall on record and a clear change of guard at the Mega slot.

Behind them, **Archaludon (20.47%, +4.46)** and **Basculegion (19.36%)** hold steady, while the Trick Room/support package is softening: **Indeedee-F dropped to 15.69% (-5.23)** and **Gholdengo to 13.85% (-5.21)**. Note the thin record — only two months (Sep, Oct) are on file for this window, so every "trend" below is a two-point line, not an established slope.

## Where it's heading

With only two data points per Pokémon, these are directional reads from a single month-over-month move, not confirmed trajectories.

The clearest momentum belongs to **Charizard-Mega-Y (+10.68)** and **Garchomp (+11.04)**, and tournament data backs them: Charizard brought 5/8 and Garchomp 4/8 at the Sitrus-Series, where a Torkoal/Venusaur sun team won. Garchomp's item profile also hardened — **Choice Scarf jumped from 32.9% to 59.8%**, with Life Orb falling to 27.4% — signaling it's being slotted as dedicated speed control, a role that tends to stick. Expect both to keep climbing or hold near their new highs.

**Salamence-Mega's -14.72 slide** is the one to watch the other way; it still won Maddo's Cup #13 and appeared 3/8 there, so it isn't dead, but the usage trajectory points down. **Venusaur (+4.50)** and **Basculegion-F (+4.72 from 0.93%)** are low-base risers riding the sun and offense surge; Basculegion-F's build also consolidated (**Focus Sash 26.6% → 79.0%**), which usually precedes further adoption. **Indeedee-F** and **Gholdengo** both shed ~5 points and look soft.

## What to prepare for

- **Sun is the new engine.** Charizard-Mega-Y (21.59%) plus Venusaur (7.21%, +4.50, 3/8 Sitrus top cut) is a real archetype now. Pack a water presence or Rock coverage; note Garchomp's Rock Slide share rose to 21.2% and Earthquake displaced Stomping Tantrum, so Garchomp is doubling as both speed control and anti-sun/anti-fire tech.
- **Expect more Scarf Garchomp.** With Choice Scarf now 59.8% of Garchomp's sets, assume it's locked into a fast move and plan your speed tiers and Protect timing accordingly.
- **Grass is still everywhere but weaker.** Rillaboom (35.52%) remains a top-2 pick and its Grassy Terrain/Fake Out core is unchanged (Miracle Seed 63.7% → 74.5%), but its -10.44 drop means Grassy Glide priority is slightly less omnipresent than September.
- **Don't over-prep for Salamence.** It fell to 12.37%; its set is stable (Salamencite 100%, Hyper Voice/Tailwind unchanged), so the decline is external competition from other Megas, not a nerf — but you can safely de-prioritize it relative to Charizard.

## Common questions

### Is Rillaboom still good in Regulation M-C?

Yes — it's still #2 at 35.52% and brought 3/8 at Maddo's Cup #13. The -10.44 drop reflects a crowded Mega/offense field pulling attention, not a change to its kit; its core moves (Fake Out, Grassy Glide, Wood Hammer) and Miracle Seed lead are intact. Treat it as a staple, just no longer the unquestioned best Pokémon in the format.

### Why is Charizard-Mega-Y usage rising?

Sun. Charizard nearly doubled to 21.59% (+10.68) and co-rose with Garchomp (+11.04) and Venusaur (+4.50), and it headlined top cut at the Sitrus-Series with a 5/8 bring rate. Its own set barely moved (Charizardite Y 100%, Heat Wave/Weather Ball stable), so the surge is adoption of the sun archetype rather than a new build.

### Should I still run Salamence-Mega?

It's viable — misoup won Maddo's Cup #13 with Salamence and it appeared 3/8 there — but its ladder usage halved to 12.37% (-14.72) as Charizard and Garchomp took over the spotlight. The build is unchanged, so if your team already supports it you lose nothing, but it's no longer the default Mega pick.
