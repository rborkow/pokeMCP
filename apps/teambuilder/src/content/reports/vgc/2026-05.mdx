## Where the meta stands

Flutter Mane remains the defining presence of Regulation F at **44.17% usage (+3.06)**, sitting more than 8 points clear of the field. The two other pillars are Urshifu-Rapid-Strike at **35.94% (-4.82)** and Incineroar at **33.46% (-0.91)**, the latter holding remarkably steady as the format's premier intimidate/redirection-bait support. Tornadus (**23.97%, -4.34**) rounds out the consistently-used speed-control and Tailwind layer.

Below the top tier, the picture is more fluid. Raging Bolt has slid to **20.25% (-6.53)** but remains the top special-attacking restricted-tier threat in this list, while Rillaboom (**19.79%, +0.64**) and Amoonguss (**17.73%, -2.68**) anchor the Grass/redirection support core. The most striking single move is Chi-Yu surging to **15.54% (+6.16)**, now the clear king of standalone special fire offense. The Ogerpon formes split duties — Hearthflame at **16.37% (-1.90)** and Wellspring at **13.04% (-4.15)** — and the Dondozo (**12.21%, +1.82**) / Tatsugiri (**11.85%, +2.35**) commander package continues to creep up. Note the sample caveat: April reflects only **20,602 battles** versus 755,266 in March, so lower-usage figures carry more noise this month.

## Where it's heading

Several risers show genuine multi-month momentum rather than one-month spikes:

- **Tatsugiri** has climbed almost every month (4.87% → 6.67% → 8.76% → 10.06% → 9.50% → **11.85%**), and with Dondozo also up to 12.21%, the commander archetype looks set to keep gaining unless directly checked.
- **Weezing-Galar** (1.55% → 7.67% over the regulation, **+4.04 this month**) and **Annihilape** (climbing since Feb to **6.17%, +3.15**) are on clear upward arcs and likely to keep rising.
- **Torkoal** (7.97% → **11.55%**) and **Iron Hands** (back up to **8.17%, +3.86**) suggest sun and trick-room/bulky-offense lines are re-emerging.
- **Chi-Yu** (+6.16) is the sharpest jump, but its prior series wobbled around 8–10% — treat the spike as real momentum but watch whether it holds past one low-volume month.

On the decline, the trends are cleaner: **Raging Bolt** has fallen every month since November (35.50% → **20.25%**), **Chien-Pao** likewise (23.25% → **13.65%**), and **Ogerpon-Wellspring** has slid five straight months (24.36% → **13.04%**). **Landorus** (-5.47) and **Dragonite** (-5.10) round out a softening of the physical-attacker layer. Extrapolating the curves, all four look likely to keep sliding.

Watch the new entrants — Chesnaught (**0.12% → 2.59%**) and Iron Moth (**0.15% → 2.06%**) jumped from near-zero in one month; that's a low-volume signal, not yet an established trend.

## What to prepare for

- **Flutter Mane is non-negotiable.** At 44.17% and rising, every team needs a reliable answer — fast priority, Sucker Punch users, or bulky Steel/Dark answers. Pairing this with the Chi-Yu surge (15.54%) means the special-attack pressure is intensifying, so bulky specially-defensive backbones earn their slot.
- **Plan for the commander package.** Dondozo (12.21%) + Tatsugiri (11.85%) are both climbing. Bring spread damage, Taunt, or knockoff/encore disruption to break the setup before Dondozo snowballs.
- **The physical-restricted layer is thinning.** Raging Bolt, Chien-Pao, Landorus, and Ogerpon-Wellspring are all in multi-month decline. You can lean slightly more into answers for the rising special and support threats (Chi-Yu, Weezing-Galar, Annihilape) rather than over-investing against fading physical attackers.
- **Account for support disruption.** Weezing-Galar (7.67%) brings Neutralizing Gas, threatening ability-dependent cores — and Incineroar (33.46%) keeps Intimidate everywhere. Don't build a team that collapses without its abilities online.

## Common questions

### Is Flutter Mane still the best Pokémon in Regulation F?

Yes — it leads at **44.17% usage, up +3.06** this month, and has topped every month on record (peaking at 49.40% in November). After dipping toward 41% in February–March, it has rebounded, so there's no sign of it losing its grip on the format.

### Why is Raging Bolt usage dropping?

Raging Bolt has declined every single month of the regulation, from **35.50% in November to 20.25% in April (-6.53 this month alone)**. The data doesn't explain the cause, but the steady, uninterrupted slide suggests the metagame has adapted to it rather than a one-off dip — expect continued decline if the trend holds.

### Is the Dondozo–Tatsugiri commander strategy good right now?

It's trending up: Tatsugiri sits at **11.85% (+2.35)** and Dondozo at **12.21% (+1.82)**, with Tatsugiri rising from under 5% at the start of the regulation. Both moving together points to a viable, growing archetype, though its low individual usage means it's a strong niche rather than a dominant pillar.
