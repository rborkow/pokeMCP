## Where the meta stands

Flutter Mane sits alone at the top on 44.17% (+3.06), reasserting itself after dipping to the low 40s in February–March. Urshifu-Rapid-Strike remains the dominant physical restricted partner at 35.94% despite a -4.82 slide, and Incineroar (33.46%, -0.91) holds its perennial pivot slot. That top three defines the format: a Fairy special threat, a water spread attacker, and the intimidate/Fake Out standard.

Below them the picture is one of consolidation around fire and speed control. Chi-Yu is the story of the month, surging +6.16 to 15.54% and jumping to #9 — its rise coincides with a set shift from Choice Specs 45.9% to Life Orb 33.0% as the top item. Tornadus (23.97%, -4.34) still anchors Tailwind, while Raging Bolt cratered -6.53 to 20.25% and Landorus fell -5.47 to 13.93%. The Chien-Pao/Chi-Yu axis diverged sharply: Chien-Pao dropped -5.00 to 13.65% as Chi-Yu climbed. Iron Hands (8.17%, +3.86) and its near-total commitment to Assault Vest (43.1% → 78.5%) round out a notable bulky-attacker resurgence.

Note the ladder volume collapse — 755,266 battles in March to 20,602 in April. April percentages sit on a far smaller sample, so treat sub-10% movers as noisier than usual.

## Where it's heading

Chi-Yu's trajectory (Mar 9.38% → Apr 15.54%) is the clearest climb, breaking out of the 8–10% band it held all regulation; if the Life Orb build keeps performing it plausibly holds double digits. Iron Hands (Mar 4.31% → Apr 8.17%) and Weezing-Galar (Mar 3.63% → Apr 7.67%) both roughly doubled month-over-month — Weezing's pivot to Covert Cloak/Choice Specs and Dazzling Gleam reflects a genuine role change, not stat noise, so continued growth is plausible. Annihilape (Mar 3.02% → Apr 6.17%) and Hatterene (Mar 3.11% → Apr 5.43%) are on similar upswings.

On the downside, Raging Bolt has fallen five straight months (35.50% in November to 20.25% now) and shows no sign of stabilizing. Landorus (18.44% → 19.40% → 13.93%) and Chien-Pao (four-month grind down to 13.65%) trend the same direction. Ogerpon-Wellspring has slid steadily since January (23.68% → 13.04%). Treat these as extrapolations from the trend, not certainties — especially given the thin April sample.

## What to prepare for

- **Fire is ascendant.** Chi-Yu (+6.16), Torkoal (+2.46), and Ogerpon-Hearthflame at 16.37% mean you should not skimp on bulk or water/rock answers. Chi-Yu's swing toward Life Orb over Choice Specs means it's staying in and clicking freely rather than locking — plan for repeated Heat Wave/Dark Pulse pressure.
- **Expect more Iron Hands, and Assault Vest specifically.** Its item share jumped from 43.1% to 78.5% AV, so it's harder to fish out with special pressure and comes with Fake Out + Wild Charge + Drain Punch. Fighting/Fairy and reliable priority help.
- **Speed control is shifting.** Tornadus (-4.34) and Chien-Pao (-5.00) both slipped, but Tornadus's Tailwind share held at 25.0% and its item moved further into Covert Cloak (47.4% → 55.2%) to dodge Fake Out/redirection. If you rely on outspeeding Tailwind teams, account for Covert Cloak neutering your disruption.
- **Weezing-Galar is a different threat now.** It abandoned Sitrus Berry (79.5% → Covert Cloak 63.0%) and added Dazzling Gleam as its top move — it's leaning into offense/utility rather than pure Neutralizing Gas walling. Don't assume the old defensive set.

## Common questions

### Is Raging Bolt still good in Reg F?

Its usage says it's fading — Raging Bolt has declined every month this regulation, from 35.50% in November to 20.25% now, a -6.53 drop in April alone. The build is essentially stable (Thunderclap 24.5% → 24.6%), though the item split moved toward Leftovers (23.3% → 33.6%) over Assault Vest, suggesting players are trying bulkier, more defensive configurations. It's still a top-five Pokémon, but the trend is unambiguously downward.

### Why is Chi-Yu suddenly everywhere?

Chi-Yu jumped +6.16 points to 15.54%, its highest mark on record after five months stuck in the 8–10% range. The mechanism is a set change: its top item flipped from Choice Specs (45.9%) to Life Orb (33.0%), giving it move flexibility instead of lock-in while retaining Heat Wave and Dark Pulse. That's a build-driven breakout rather than a passive drift.

### Should I still run Chien-Pao?

Chien-Pao dropped -5.00 to 13.65%, continuing a decline from 23.25% in November. Its set barely changed — Focus Sash actually rose to 89.8% — so the fall is external, coinciding with Chi-Yu's rise as the preferred Dark-type restricted-tier attacker. It's still viable and top-12, but the format is currently rewarding Chi-Yu over it.
